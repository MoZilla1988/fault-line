Field Guide

The job

Faults travel down the feeders toward the main switchgear. Each one that reaches it costs grid integrity, and at zero the site blacks out. Clear all 10 waves, including the boss on wave 10, to unlock the next site. The game pauses while this guide is open.

Building

  • Drag a tower from the tray onto a pad, or tap it, tap a pad, and confirm.
  • Tap a placed tower to upgrade, guard, replace a fuse, or sell (70% back). Drag it to move it.
  • Press Energize when you're ready. You earn a bonus after every wave.

Power

  • Towers only fire inside a supply ring. The switchgear has one, and each generator adds a big one.
  • Voltage drops toward the edge of a ring, down to 75% damage.
  • Every tower draws kW from the bus. Generators raise the bus capacity.
  • During a utility outage only towers inside a generator ring keep firing.

Selective coordination

Place a Fuse upstream of a Breaker (farther from the main, within 120px). Both get +25% damage and a green C badge.

Enemies and counters

Towers

Quick tips

  • Put damage towers at feeder bends so enemies stay in range longer.
  • Check the intel card before each wave. Red items will cost you integrity.
  • Guard your generators before squirrel waves. A shorted generator drops its whole ring.
  • Save credits for wave 9 so you can prepare for the boss.
